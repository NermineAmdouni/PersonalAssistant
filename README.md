# PersonalAssistant
This project is very close to my heart. A personal assistant for all my fellow neurodivergents and anxious pals. I'm trying to create an app that is free and can assist you with your routine, your tasks, social life, big goals and even just remembering to eat. I am open to any suggestions .

Have you ever dreamed of having a personal assistant that walks around with you just taking notes of your tasks and reminds you of them, plans your day and helps you prioritize the millions thoughts and ideas in your head. Well that's what i'm trying to achieve with this project for you guys (and me <3).

## Project Goals
The goals i'm focusing on with this assistant is to create:

1. Connect to Google Calendar so the assistant can read events and tasks, send reminders,
and add items.
2. Break tasks down into small, concrete steps.
3. Turn goals into plans that fit how ADHD brains work (short steps, flexible timing, visible
progress).
4. Help prioritize by sorting tasks into categories like urgent vs. not urgent and in my control
vs. not in my control.
5. Build routines around the person's real life (hygiene, meals, work, social) and remind them
gently.
6. Add small tools like a Pomodoro timer and fidget games, Emotions wheel and regulation.
