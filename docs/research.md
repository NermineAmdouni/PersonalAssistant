# Research

## Problem Statement
Most apps i tried on the app store or online focus on only one aspect of ADHD. Some are for simply planning and tracking progress or habits. Others are just made to simplify taks and break them into small steps.
Some are chatbots that work as councelling or there for emotional support. None of them have access to your calendar which is where most people actually plan tehir jobs and save their meetings and online work, their gfriends birthdays ect...
And of course most of these apps are fully free when they can provide more than one service.

Technical Goals:
- Make this available and linked to your phone and laptop browser (preferably opera then add otehrs along the way) as an extension.
- Third-Party like friends family and partners can add to the list to help. (Ticktick app)
- The assistant has access to the user calendar and can add to it through you asking it to directly or you mentioning it to the chatbot (the chatbot would of course ask for permission before adding and deleting things from your tasks and calendar)
- Make the chahtbot safe to use does not make personal life decisions for you for your personal life or actual job.
